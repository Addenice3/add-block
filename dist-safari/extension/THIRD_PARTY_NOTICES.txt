add-block third-party notices
=============================

The rulesets in rulesets/ are converted from these filter lists:

  EasyList     (rulesets/easylist.json)
  EasyPrivacy  (rulesets/easyprivacy.json)

  Copyright: The EasyList authors, https://easylist.to/
  Licence:   dual licensed, at your option, under
             - GNU General Public License version 3 or later
               https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html
             - Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported or later
               https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
             See https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html

  Changes:   the filters were converted to Chrome declarativeNetRequest rules
             with abp2dnr; cosmetic and other non-network filters were left
             out, and rules that differ only in host were merged. The exact
             list versions (source URL, date, SHA-256) are pinned in
             lists.lock.json in the add-block source repository.

The URLs above are for reference only. The extension never loads them.
